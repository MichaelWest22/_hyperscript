🍼 Dyperscript 🍼

Hyperscript for toddlers! (the underscore is still silent, but now it giggles)

What is Dyperscript?
Dyperscript is a toddler-friendly variant of hyperscript that uses baby language keywords instead of grown-up words. Perfect for when you want your code to be as cute as a button! 👶

🎯 Basic Click Actions

Instead of boring "on click toggle", we say "when ouchie make"!

Dyperscript: dy="when ouchie make red-thingy"
Converts to: _="on click toggle .red"
🎈

Poke the balloon!

👋 Show and Hide (Peekaboo!)

Dyperscript: dy="when touchy peekaboo #secret-box"
Converts to: _="on click show #secret-box"
🎁

🎪 Fun Animations

Dyperscript: dy="when ouchie make bouncy"
Converts to: _="on click toggle .bounce"
🌟

😊 Mood Changes

Dyperscript: dy="when boop flip happy and sad"
Converts to: _="on click toggle .happy .sad"
😐

⏰ Wait Commands (Patience, little one!)

Dyperscript: dy="when ouchie make blue-thingy wait-wait quick next make red-thingy"
Converts to: _="on click toggle .blue wait 100ms then toggle .red"
🎨
